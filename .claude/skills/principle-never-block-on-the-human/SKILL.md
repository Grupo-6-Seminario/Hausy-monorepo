# Work independently within scope

Perform discoverable facts, routine implementation choices, and reversible verification without repeatedly asking permission. Human decisions remain the new-feature brief, changed scope or acceptance criteria, and the approval boundaries in `docs/agents/workflow.md`.

A user telling you to continue does not supply missing approval for those decisions. Keep independent work moving while an unresolved decision waits; do not implement dependent behavior until settled.
