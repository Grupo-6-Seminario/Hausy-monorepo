# Research

Define the factual question and the sources that own its answer. Inspect official documentation, source code, specifications, or first-party data. Follow claims to their original source. Delegate one bounded investigation if useful under `docs/agents/workflow.md`.

Write a concise local findings document with direct citations, observation dates for changing facts, and clear separation of evidence, interpretation, assumptions, and unknowns. Flag contradictory sources and unresolved gaps. Research does not authorize paid evaluations, account authentication, issue publication, messaging, or implementation beyond scope.
