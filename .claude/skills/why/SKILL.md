# Why

Anchor the question to concrete behavior or code. Inspect relevant ADRs, glossary, commit history, tests, and available discussion. Use one bounded investigator if useful; follow `docs/agents/workflow.md`.

Explain the chosen tradeoff and credible alternatives. Label inferred rationale when no recorded decision exists. Preserve disagreement; do not fabricate historical certainty. Cite the evidence that supports the explanation and what would change it. Keep outputs local.
